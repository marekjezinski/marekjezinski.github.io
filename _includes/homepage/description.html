The FIZZ (Fanciful, Improbable, Zany Zephyrs) Symposium celebrates whimsical approaches to computation: pancake-sorting quantum squirrels, provably optimal procrastination algorithms, and the formal semantics of imaginary numbers that insist on being polite. Research themes include approximate unicorn enumeration, constructive daydreaming, and runtime verification for sentient coffee machines.

This year's conference is hosted in the Virtual Treehouse at the Institute for Made-Up Sciences. Scheduled events include workshops on "Formalising Nonsense", "Probabilistic Tea Leaves for Machine Learning", and a hackathon to build a certified sarcasm detector.
